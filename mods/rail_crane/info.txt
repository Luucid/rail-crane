[STEAM_TITLE:Crane]
[STEAM_DESCRIPTION:Adds a crane that builds walls. Place it on dry ground or on level minecart track. Rolling cranes can follow bends and loops. Choose a boom height, then click the finished crane to mark the tops of wall columns. It builds from the ground, seabed, or existing walls. For magma, build the crane with a magma-safe chain such as iron or steel. Requires DFHack. Tested on Dwarf Fortress Steam 53.17 with DFHack 53.17-r1. I used AI to help write the code and make the crane graphics.]
